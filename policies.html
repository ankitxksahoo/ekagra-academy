Policies.

Plain-language terms for families. If anything here is unclear, call admissions and we will explain it.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry and to teach your child: the student's name, class, school and board, a parent's name, mobile number and email, and later, attendance and test scores.

Why we use it

  • To call you back about an enquiry or a demo class you requested.
  • To register a student for EKAT and send the admit card and result.
  • To run classes, tests and parent reports after admission.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

Consent and children's data

We contact you only after you agree to it on a form. Because most of our students are under 18, we ask a parent or guardian to give that consent. You can withdraw it at any time by replying STOP to any message or by writing to admissions@ekagra.example.

Who we share it with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the institute, such as SMS and payment providers, and with a lending partner if you choose to pay in instalments through them. Each is bound by contract to use it only for that purpose.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to admission are deleted after 24 months. Student records are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or erase your data, or raise a grievance, by writing to our grievance officer at admissions@ekagra.example. We respond within 30 days.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Terms of admission

  • Admission is confirmed when the first fee instalment and the required documents are received.
  • Batches are allotted on the basis of EKAT or an entry assessment. Batch and centre transfers depend on seat availability.
  • Classes are not held during school hours, and every batch has one weekly off.
  • Study material and class recordings are for the enrolled student's personal use and may not be shared or resold.
  • We do not guarantee any rank, score or admission. Results depend on each student's effort, and no coaching institute can promise them.
  • The fee stated in the admission letter stays fixed for the full duration of the course.

Refunds

  • Before the batch starts: everything paid is refunded, less a processing charge of ₹1,000.
  • After the batch starts: the fee for the remaining period is refunded on a pro-rata basis within 10 days of a written request.
  • Printed study material already issued is not refundable.
  • For instalment plans through a lending partner, the refund is settled with the lender first.
  • Refunds are calculated on the amount actually paid, after any scholarship.

Results and testimonials

Every result we publish names the program the student took, how long they studied with us and their centre. We publish a student's name, photo or words only with written consent from the student and a parent, and we never pay for testimonials. Scorecards for published results can be seen at our Kothrud office.
