EKAT questions.

Anything else? Call admissions on +91 98765 43210.

Is EKAT really free?

Yes. Registration, the test and the result are free. There is no obligation to join after the test.

Can I take EKAT more than once?

You can attempt it on any two of the three dates. We use your better score for the scholarship.

What syllabus does the test follow?

Questions come from the class you are in now, up to the chapters most schools finish by October. Mental ability questions need no preparation.

How do I take the online test?

You get a login by SMS two days before the test. You need a laptop or a tablet with a webcam and a steady internet connection. The test is proctored.

When will I get my result?

Within 7 days, by SMS and email, with your score, your rank among all candidates and your scholarship.

Does the scholarship apply for both years of a two-year program?

Yes, as long as attendance stays above 85% and the student takes all Sunday tests.
