Questions parents ask.

Still deciding? A counsellor can walk you through programs, fees and batches in ten minutes.

What does "integrated" mean at Ekagra?

Your child prepares for school boards and for JEE or NEET in a single timetable. In Class 11 and 12, our classes are timed around junior college hours and our board teaching follows the CBSE, ICSE and HSC syllabus, so school and entrance preparation never pull in different directions.

How big are the batches?

A maximum of 32 students. When a batch is full we open a new one with its own teachers rather than adding chairs.

Can my child switch between classroom and online classes?

Yes. Every classroom lecture is also streamed and recorded on the Ekagra app, so a student who is unwell or travelling can join live from home. You can change your mode once a term.

How does the EKAT scholarship work?

EKAT is a free admission and talent test held on 8 November, 22 November and 6 December 2026. Your score decides your scholarship, from 10% up to 90% of tuition. Results come out within 7 days.

What are the fees, and is there an EMI option?

Fees start at ₹36,000 a year for Board Excellence and ₹1,64,000 a year for Integrated JEE, plus 18% GST. You can pay in full (5% off tuition), in two instalments, or in 10 monthly instalments through our partner lenders. The course finder shows the exact amount for your choices.

What happens if a student leaves mid-course?

The fee for the remaining period is refunded on a pro-rata basis within 10 days of the request, as set out in our refund policy. Fees never increase during a course.
